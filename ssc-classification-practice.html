1. Word Classification – 8 Questions

Question 1 Basic
Select the odd word out.
(A) Mango
(B) Apple
(C) Banana
(D) Carrot
Answer: (D) Carrot
Solution: Mango, Apple and Banana are fruits, whereas Carrot is a vegetable.
Question 2 Basic
Select the odd one out.
(A) Mercury
(B) Venus
(C) Earth
(D) Moon
Answer: (D) Moon
Solution: Mercury, Venus and Earth are planets. Moon is a natural satellite.
Question 3 Basic
Select the odd word out.
(A) Triangle
(B) Square
(C) Circle
(D) Cube
Answer: (D) Cube
Solution: Triangle, Square and Circle are plane figures (2-D), whereas Cube is a solid figure (3-D).
Question 4 Basic
Select the word that is different from the other three.
(A) January
(B) March
(C) July
(D) Monday
Answer: (D) Monday
Solution: January, March and July are months. Monday is a day of the week.

3. Alphabet / Letter Classification – 8 Questions

Question 24 Moderate
Find the odd letter group.
(A) BDF
(B) HJL
(C) NPR
(D) TVY
Answer: (D) TVY
Solution: BDF, HJL and NPR have gaps of +2 and +2. TVY has positions 20, 22, 25, so the last gap is different.
Question 25 Moderate
Select the odd letter group.
(A) CFI
(B) LOR
(C) UXA
(D) GJN
Answer: (D) GJN
Solution: CFI, LOR and UXA move by +3 letters cyclically. GJN has G → J = +3, J → N = +4, so it is different.
Question 26 Moderate
Which pair is different from the other three?
(A) AZ
(B) BY
(C) CX
(D) EX
Answer: (D) EX
Solution: In AZ, BY and CX, the alphabet positions add to 27: 1+26, 2+25 and 3+24. E+X = 5+24 = 29.
Question 27 Moderate
Find the odd letter group.
(A) ADG
(B) HKN
(C) PSV
(D) TVX
Answer: (D) TVX
Solution: ADG, HKN and PSV have a constant gap of +3 between letters. TVX has gaps of +2 and +2. Therefore, D is different.
Question 28 Advanced
Select the odd group.
(A) ACEG
(B) IKMO
(C) QSUV
(D) TWYZ
Answer: Question needs review
Solution: ACEG and IKMO move by +2 at every step. QSUV has gaps +2, +2, +1, while TWYZ has gaps +3, +2, +1. Both C and D differ from the +2 pattern, so the question does not have a unique answer as written.
Question 29 Advanced
Find the odd group.
(A) BGLQ
(B) CHMR
(C) DINS
(D) EJOX
Answer: (D) EJOX
Solution: BGLQ, CHMR and DINS each move by +5 positions. In EJOX, E→J→O is +5 but O→X is +9.
Question 30 Advanced
Which pair is different?
(A) AB
(B) EF
(C) IJ
(D) MN
Answer: No unique odd pair
Solution: All four options are consecutive-letter pairs. Their starting letters A, E, I and M are separated by four alphabet positions, so MN continues the same pattern. The question needs a revised option to have one uniquely correct answer.
Question 31 Advanced
Find the odd letter group.
(A) ZXV
(B) RPN
(C) JHF
(D) DCB
Answer: (D) DCB
Solution: ZXV, RPN and JHF decrease by 2 positions at each step. DCB has D→C = -1 and C→B = -1.

4. Number-Set Classification – 8 Questions

Question 32 Advanced
Three of the following number sets follow the same relation. Find the odd set.
(A) (14, 57, 29)
(B) (18, 73, 37)
(C) (25, 101, 51)
(D) (13, 52, 27)
Answer: (D) (13, 52, 27)
Solution:
In A, B and C:
Second number = 4 × First + 1
Third number = 2 × First + 1

For D: 4 × 13 + 1 = 53, not 52. Therefore D is the odd set.
Question 33 Advanced

(D) (17, 53, 19)

Question 40 Moderate

(A) Triangle

(B) 3

(D) 9

Question 42 Moderate
Find the odd number.
(A) 1
(B) 4
(C) 9
(D) 15
Answer: (D) 15
Solution: 1 = 1², 4 = 2² and 9 = 3². 15 is not a perfect square.
Question 43 Moderate
Which one is different from the other three?
(A) Keyboard
(B) Mouse
(C) Monitor
(D) Notebook
Answer: (D) Notebook
Solution: Keyboard, Mouse and Monitor are computer hardware devices. Notebook is a stationery item.
Question 44 Advanced
Find the odd pair.
(A) 3 : 9
(B) 4 : 16
(C) 5 : 25
(D) 6 : 30
Answer: (D) 6 : 30
Solution: In A, B and C, the second number is the square of the first: 3² = 9, 4² = 16 and 5² = 25. 6² = 36, not 30.
Question 45 Advanced

(D) 16 : 36
Answer: Question needs correction
Solution: The original options do not follow a consistent rule for A, B and C. A corrected pattern could use (7,16), (10,22), (13,28) and (16,34), where the second number is 2 × the first + 2. The original question needs correction before being used as a verified exam question.

6. SSC Exam-Level Higher Classification – 5 Questions

Question 46 Exam Level
Three pairs follow the same relation. Find the odd pair.
(A) 2 : 8
(B) 3 : 27
(C) 4 : 64
(D) 5 : 100
Answer: (D) 5 : 100
Solution: In A, B and C, the second number is the cube of the first: 2³ = 8, 3³ = 27 and 4³ = 64. 5³ = 125, not 100.
Question 47 Exam Level
Find the odd number set.
(A) (17, 12, 7)
(B) (15, 19, 23)
(C) (21, 18, 15)
(D) (14, 18, 20)
Answer: (D) (14, 18, 20)
Solution: In A, B and C, First + Third = 2 × Second.
A: 17+7 = 24 = 2×12
B: 15+23 = 38 = 2×19
C: 21+15 = 36 = 2×18
D: 14+20 = 34, whereas 2×18 = 36.
Question 48 Exam Level
Which sequence is different from the other three?
(A) 3, 6, 12, 24
(B) 5, 10, 20, 40
(C) 7, 14, 28, 56
(D) 9, 18, 35, 72
Answer: (D) 9, 18, 35, 72
Solution: In A, B and C, every term is multiplied by 2. In D, 18×2 = 36, not 35.
Question 49 Exam Level
Select the odd pair.
(A) 3 : 12
(B) 5 : 30
(C) 7 : 56
(D) 9 : 81
Answer: (D) 9 : 81
Solution: In A, B and C, Second = First × (First + 1). 3×4=12, 5×6=30, 7×8=56. For 9, the expected value is 9×10 = 90, not 81.
Question 50 Exam Level
Three number sets follow the same rule. Find the odd set.
(A) (8, 15, 9)
(B) (5, 14, 13)
(C) (7, 16, 9)
(D) (6, 14, 11)
Answer: (D) (6, 14, 11)
Solution: Compare the sum of each set.
A: 8+15+9 = 32
B: 5+14+13 = 32
C: 7+16+9 = 32
D: 6+14+11 = 31
Therefore, D is the odd set because its total is 31, while the other three totals are 32.

📌 Practice Strategy

Classification questions should be solved by identifying the exact common property shared by three options and then locating the option that does not follow that property.

For SSC-level preparation, pay special attention to number relations, alphabet positions, mathematical patterns and three-number set relations.
