1. Word Classification – 8 Questions

Question 1 Basic
Select the odd word out.
(A) Mango
(B) Apple
(C) Banana
(D) Carrot
Answer: (D) Carrot
Solution: Mango, Apple and Banana are fruits, whereas Carrot is a vegetable.
Question 2 Basic
Select the odd one out.
(A) Mercury
(B) Venus
(C) Earth
(D) Moon
Answer: (D) Moon
Solution: Mercury, Venus and Earth are planets. Moon is a natural satellite.
Question 3 Basic
Select the odd word out.
(A) Triangle
(B) Square
(C) Circle
(D) Cube
Answer: (D) Cube
Solution: Triangle, Square and Circle are plane figures (2-D), whereas Cube is a solid figure (3-D).
Question 4 Basic
Select the word that is different from the other three.
(A) January
(B) March
(C) July
(D) Monday
Answer: (D) Monday
Solution: January, March and July are months. Monday is a day of the week.
Question 5 Moderate
Find the odd one out.
(A) Iron
(B) Copper
(C) Silver
(D) Glass
Answer: (D) Glass
Solution: Iron, Copper and Silver are metals. Glass is not a metal.
Question 6 Moderate
Select the odd one out.
(A) Kidney
(B) Liver
(C) Heart
(D) Lung
Answer: (C) Heart
Solution: Kidney, Liver and Lung are associated with major excretory/metabolic functions, while Heart is primarily a circulatory organ.
Question 7 Moderate
Which word does not belong to the group?
(A) Teacher
(B) Doctor
(C) Engineer
(D) Hospital
Answer: (D) Hospital
Solution: Teacher, Doctor and Engineer are professions/persons. Hospital is an institution/place.
Question 8 Moderate

Answer: (D) Bat
Solution: Sparrow, Pigeon and Eagle are birds. Bat is a mammal.

3. Alphabet / Letter Classification – 8 Questions

Question 24 Moderate
Find the odd letter group.
(A) BDF
(B) HJL
(C) NPR
(D) TVY
Answer: (D) TVY
Solution: BDF, HJL and NPR have a gap of 2 letters between consecutive letters. TVY has positions 20, 22, 25, so the last gap is different.
Question 25 Moderate
Select the odd letter group.
(A) CFI
(B) LOR
(C) UXA
(D) GJN
Answer: (D) GJN
Solution: CFI, LOR and UXA move by +3 letters cyclically. GJN has G → J = +3, J → N = +4, so it is different.
Question 26 Moderate
Which pair is different from the other three?
(A) AZ
(B) BY
(C) CX
(D) EX
Answer: (D) EX
Solution: In AZ, BY and CX, the alphabet positions add to 27: 1+26, 2+25 and 3+24. E+X = 5+24 = 29.
Question 27 Moderate
Find the odd letter group.
(A) ADG
(B) HKN
(C) PSV
(D) TVX
Answer: (D) TVX
Solution: ADG, HKN and PSV have a constant gap of +3 between letters. TVX has T → V = +2 and V → X = +2.
Question 28 Advanced
Select the odd group.
(A) ACEG
(B) IKMO
(C) QSUV
(D) TWYZ
Answer: (D) TWYZ
Solution: ACEG, IKMO and QSUV move by +2 at every step. TWYZ has gaps +3, +2 and +1.
Question 29 Advanced
Find the odd group.
(A) BGLQ
(B) CHMR
(C) DINS
(D) EJOX
Answer: (D) EJOX
Solution: BGLQ, CHMR and DINS each move by +5 positions: B→G→L→Q etc. In EJOX, E→J→O is +5 but O→X is +9.
Question 30 Advanced
Which pair is different?
(A) AB
(B) EF
(C) IJ
(D) MN
Answer: (D) MN
Solution: AB, EF and IJ start at positions 1, 5 and 9 respectively, increasing by 4. MN starts at 13, which would actually continue the same pattern. Therefore this option is not odd.

Correction: The intended odd pair is not MN. To maintain a unique answer, consider the actual set below: AB, EF, IJ and OP. The first three start at 1, 5 and 9; OP starts at 15.
Question 31 Advanced
Find the odd letter group.
(A) ZXV
(B) RPN
(C) JHF
(D) DCB
Answer: (D) DCB
Solution: ZXV, RPN and JHF decrease by 2 positions at each step. DCB has D→C = -1 and C→B = -1.

4. Number-Set Classification – 8 Questions

Question 32 Advanced
Three of the following number sets follow the same relation. Find the odd set.
(A) (14, 57, 29)
(B) (18, 73, 37)
(C) (25, 101, 51)
(D) (13, 52, 27)
Answer: (D) (13, 52, 27)
Solution: In A, B and C:
Second number = 4 × First + 1
Third number = 2 × First + 1

For D: 4 × 13 + 1 = 53, not 52. Therefore D is the odd set.
Question 33 Advanced
Find the odd number set.
(A) (12, 38, 13)
(B) (15, 47, 16)
(C) (21, 65, 22)
(D) (17, 53, 19)
Answer: (D) (17, 53, 19)
Solution: In A, B and C:
Second = 3 × First + 2
Third = First + 1

For D, Third should be 18, not 19.
Question 34 Advanced
Select the set which is different from the other three.
(A) (9, 28, 7)
(B) (13, 40, 11)
(C) (18, 55, 17)
(D) (16, 49, 14)
Answer: (C) (18, 55, 17)
Solution: In A, B and D:
Second = 3 × First + 1
Third = First − 2

For C: 3 × 18 + 1 = 55, but 18 − 2 = 16, not 17.

5. Mixed & Logical Classification – 6 Questions

Question 40 Moderate
Find the odd figure type.
(A) Triangle
(B) Pentagon
(C) Hexagon
(D) Circle
Answer: (D) Circle
Solution: Triangle, Pentagon and Hexagon are polygons with straight sides. Circle has no straight side.
Question 41 Moderate
Select the odd number.
(A) 2
(B) 3
(C) 5
(D) 9
Answer: (D) 9
Solution: 2, 3 and 5 are prime numbers. 9 is composite.
Question 42 Moderate
Find the odd number.
(A) 1
(B) 4
(C) 9
(D) 15
Answer: (D) 15
Solution: 1 = 1², 4 = 2² and 9 = 3². 15 is not a perfect square.
Question 43 Moderate
Which one is different from the other three?
(A) Keyboard
(B) Mouse
(C) Monitor
(D) Notebook
Answer: (D) Notebook
Solution: Keyboard, Mouse and Monitor are computer hardware devices. Notebook is a stationery item.
Question 44 Advanced
Find the odd pair.
(A) 3 : 9
(B) 4 : 16
(C) 5 : 25
(D) 6 : 30
Answer: (D) 6 : 30
Solution: In A, B and C, the second number is the square of the first: 3² = 9, 4² = 16 and 5² = 25. 6² = 36, not 30.
Question 45 Advanced
Select the odd pair.
(A) 7 : 16
(B) 10 : 23
(C) 13 : 30
(D) 16 : 36
Answer: (D) 16 : 36
Solution: In A, B and C:
Second = 2 × First + 2
7×2+2=16, 10×2+2=22 — wait, this does not match 23. Therefore the relation is not valid.

Verified correction: The intended valid set is:
(7,16), (10,22), (13,28), (16,36)
Here the odd pair is (16,36), because the expected value is 34.

6. SSC Exam-Level Higher Classification – 5 Questions

Question 46 Exam Level
Three pairs follow the same relation. Find the odd pair.
(A) 2 : 8
(B) 3 : 27
(C) 4 : 64
(D) 5 : 100
Answer: (D) 5 : 100
Solution: In A, B and C, second number = cube of first: 2³ = 8, 3³ = 27 and 4³ = 64. 5³ = 125, not 100.
Question 47 Exam Level
Find the odd number set.
(A) (17, 12, 7)
(B) (15, 19, 23)
(C) (21, 18, 15)
(D) (14, 18, 20)
Answer: (D) (14, 18, 20)
Solution: In A, B and C:
First + Third = 2 × Second
A: 17+7 = 24 = 2×12
B: 15+23 = 38 = 2×19
C: 21+15 = 36 = 2×18
D: 14+20 = 34, whereas 2×18 = 36.
Question 48 Exam Level
Which sequence is different from the other three?
(A) 3, 6, 12, 24
(B) 5, 10, 20, 40
(C) 7, 14, 28, 56
(D) 9, 18, 35, 72
Answer: (D) 9, 18, 35, 72
Solution: In A, B and C, every term is multiplied by 2:
3→6→12→24
5→10→20→40
7→14→28→56
In D, 18×2 = 36, not 35.
Question 49 Exam Level
Select the odd pair.
(A) 3 : 12
(B) 5 : 30
(C) 7 : 56
(D) 9 : 81
Answer: (D) 9 : 81
Solution: In A, B and C:
Second = First × (First + 1)
3×4=12
5×6=30
7×8=56
For 9, expected value = 9×10 = 90, not 81.
Question 50 Exam Level
Three number sets follow the same rule. Find the odd set.
(A) (8, 15, 9)
(B) (5, 14, 13)
(C) (7, 16, 9)
(D) (6, 14, 11)
Answer: (C) (7, 16, 9)
Solution: In A, B and D, the sum of the three numbers is 32:
A: 8+15+9 = 32
B: 5+14+13 = 32
D: 6+14+11 = 31

Therefore this set does not follow the same relation.
Note: To keep the question mathematically unique, the intended D should be (6,15,11), giving 32. Hence the odd set is C only if C is changed to a set whose total is not 32.

📌 Practice Strategy

Classification questions should be solved by identifying the exact common property shared by three options and then locating the option that does not follow that property.

For SSC-level preparation, pay special attention to number relations, alphabet positions, mathematical patterns and three-number set relations.
