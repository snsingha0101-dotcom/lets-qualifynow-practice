1. Word Classification – 8 Questions

Question 1Basic
Select the odd word out.
(A) Mango
(B) Apple
(C) Banana
(D) Carrot
Answer: (D) Carrot
Solution: Mango, Apple and Banana are fruits, whereas Carrot is a vegetable.
Question 2Basic
Select the odd one out.
(A) Mercury
(B) Venus
(C) Earth
(D) Moon
Answer: (D) Moon
Solution: Mercury, Venus and Earth are planets. Moon is a natural satellite.
Question 3Basic
Select the odd word out.
(A) Triangle
(B) Square
(C) Circle
(D) Cube
Answer: (D) Cube
Solution: Triangle, Square and Circle are plane figures (2-D), whereas Cube is a solid figure (3-D).
Question 4Basic
Select the word that is different from the other three.
(A) January
(B) March
(C) July
(D) Monday
Answer: (D) Monday
Solution: January, March and July are months. Monday is a day of the week.
Question 5Moderate
Find the odd one out.
(A) Iron
(B) Copper
(C) Silver
(D) Glass
Answer: (D) Glass
Solution: Iron, Copper and Silver are metals. Glass is not a metal.
Question 6Moderate
Select the odd one out.
(A) Kidney
(B) Liver
(C) Heart
(D) Lung
Answer: (C) Heart
Solution: Kidney, Liver and Lung are involved in important metabolic, respiratory or excretory functions. Heart is primarily responsible for pumping blood through the circulatory system.
Question 7Moderate
Which word does not belong to the group?
(A) Teacher
(B) Doctor
(C) Engineer
(D) Hospital
Answer: (D) Hospital
Solution: Teacher, Doctor and Engineer are professions/persons. Hospital is an institution/place.

Solution: Sparrow, Pigeon and Eagle are birds. Bat is a mammal.

3. Alphabet / Letter Classification – 8 Questions

Question 24Moderate
Find the odd letter group.
(A) BDF
(B) HJL
(C) NPR
(D) TVY
Answer: (D) TVY
Solution: BDF, HJL and NPR have gaps of +2 and +2. TVY has positions 20, 22, 25, so the last gap is different.
Question 25Moderate
Select the odd letter group.
(A) CFI
(B) LOR
(C) UXA
(D) GJN
Answer: (D) GJN
Solution: CFI, LOR and UXA move by +3 letters cyclically. GJN has G → J = +3, J → N = +4, so it is different.
Question 26Moderate
Which pair is different from the other three?
(A) AZ
(B) BY
(C) CX
(D) EX
Answer: (D) EX
Solution: In AZ, BY and CX, the alphabet positions add to 27: 1+26, 2+25 and 3+24. E+X = 5+24 = 29.
Question 27Moderate
Find the odd letter group.
(A) ADG
(B) HKN
(C) PSV
(D) TVX
Answer: (D) TVX
Solution: ADG, HKN and PSV have a constant gap of +3 between letters. TVX has gaps of +2 and +2. Therefore, D is different.
Question 28Advanced
Select the odd group.
(A) ACEG
(B) IKMO
(C) QSUW
(D) TWYZ
Answer: (D) TWYZ
Solution: ACEG, IKMO and QSUW each move forward by +2 alphabet positions at every step. TWYZ has gaps of +3, +2 and +1, so it is the odd group.
Question 29Advanced
Find the odd group.
(A) BGLQ
(B) CHMR
(C) DINS
(D) EJOX
Answer: (D) EJOX
Solution: BGLQ, CHMR and DINS each move by +5 positions. In EJOX, E→J→O is +5 but O→X is +9.
Question 30Advanced
Which pair is different?
(A) AB
(B) EF
(C) IJ
(D) MO
Answer: (D) MO
Solution: AB, EF and IJ are consecutive-letter pairs. M and O have one letter between them, so MO does not follow the same pattern.
Question 31Advanced
Find the odd letter group.
(A) ZXV
(B) RPN
(C) JHF
(D) DCB
Answer: (D) DCB
Solution: ZXV, RPN and JHF decrease by 2 positions at each step. DCB has D→C = −1 and C→B = −1.

4. Number-Set Classification – 8 Questions

Question 32Advanced
Three of the following number sets follow the same relation. Find the odd set.
(A) (14, 57, 29)
(B) (18, 73, 37)
(C) (25, 101, 51)
(D) (13, 52, 27)
Answer: (D) (13, 52, 27)
Solution:
In A, B and C:
Second number = 4 × First + 1
Third number = 2 × First + 1

For D: 4 × 13 + 1 = 53, not 52. Therefore D is the odd set.
Question 33Advanced
Find the odd number set.
(A) (12, 38, 13)
(B) (15, 47, 16)
(C) (21, 65, 22)
(D) (17, 53, 19)
Answer: (D) (17, 53, 19)
Solution: In A, B and C, Second = 3 × First + 2 and Third = First + 1. For D, the third number should be 18, not 19.
Question 34Advanced
Select the set which is different from the other three.
(A) (9, 28, 7)
(B) (13, 40, 11)
(C) (18, 55, 17)
(D) (16, 49, 14)
Answer: (C) (18, 55, 17)
Solution: In A, B and D, Second = 3 × First + 1 and Third = First − 2. For C, 18 − 2 = 16, not 17.
Question 35Advanced
Find the odd number set.

(B) (7, 50, 14)
(C) (9, 82, 18)
(D) (8, 66, 16)

Question 36Advanced

Question 38Advanced

5. Mixed & Logical Classification – 6 Questions

Question 40Moderate
Find the odd figure type.
(A) Triangle
(B) Pentagon
(C) Hexagon
(D) Circle
Answer: (D) Circle
Solution: Triangle, Pentagon and Hexagon are polygons with straight sides. Circle has no straight side.
Question 41Moderate
Select the odd number.
(A) 2
(B) 3
(C) 5
(D) 9
Answer: (D) 9
Solution: 2, 3 and 5 are prime numbers. 9 is composite.
Question 42Moderate
Find the odd number.
(A) 1
(B) 4
(C) 9
(D) 15
Answer: (D) 15
Solution: 1 = 1², 4 = 2² and 9 = 3². 15 is not a perfect square.
Question 43Moderate
Which one is different from the other three?
(A) Keyboard
(B) Mouse
(C) Monitor
(D) Notebook
Answer: (D) Notebook
Solution: Keyboard, Mouse and Monitor are computer hardware devices. Notebook is a stationery item.
Question 44Advanced
Find the odd pair.
(A) 3 : 9
(B) 4 : 16
(C) 5 : 25
(D) 6 : 30
Answer: (D) 6 : 30
Solution: In A, B and C, the second number is the square of the first: 3² = 9, 4² = 16 and 5² = 25. 6² = 36, not 30.
Question 45Advanced
Select the odd pair.
(A) 7 : 16
(B) 10 : 22
(C) 13 : 28
(D) 16 : 36
Answer: (D) 16 : 36
Solution: In A, B and C, Second = 2 × First + 2. For A, 2 × 7 + 2 = 16; for B, 2 × 10 + 2 = 22; for C, 2 × 13 + 2 = 28. For D, the expected number is 2 × 16 + 2 = 34, not 36.

6. SSC Exam-Level Higher Classification – 5 Questions

Question 46Exam Level
Three pairs follow the same relation. Find the odd pair.
(A) 2 : 8
(B) 3 : 27
(C) 4 : 64
(D) 5 : 100
Answer: (D) 5 : 100
Solution: In A, B and C, the second number is the cube of the first: 2³ = 8, 3³ = 27 and 4³ = 64. 5³ = 125, not 100.
Question 47Exam Level
Find the odd number set.
(A) (17, 12, 7)
(B) (15, 19, 23)
(C) (21, 18, 15)
(D) (14, 18, 20)
Answer: (D) (14, 18, 20)
Solution: In A, B and C, First + Third = 2 × Second.
A: 17 + 7 = 24 = 2 × 12
B: 15 + 23 = 38 = 2 × 19
C: 21 + 15 = 36 = 2 × 18
D: 14 + 20 = 34, whereas 2 × 18 = 36.
Question 48Exam Level
Which sequence is different from the other three?
(A) 3, 6, 12, 24
(B) 5, 10, 20, 40
(C) 7, 14, 28, 56
(D) 9, 18, 35, 72
Answer: (D) 9, 18, 35, 72
Solution: In A, B and C, every term is multiplied by 2. In D, 18 × 2 = 36, not 35.
Question 49Exam Level
Select the odd pair.
(A) 3 : 12
(B) 5 : 30
(C) 7 : 56
(D) 9 : 81
Answer: (D) 9 : 81
Solution: In A, B and C, Second = First × (First + 1). 3 × 4 = 12, 5 × 6 = 30 and 7 × 8 = 56. For 9, the expected value is 9 × 10 = 90, not 81.
Question 50Exam Level
Three number sets follow the same rule. Find the odd set.
(A) (8, 15, 9)
(B) (5, 14, 13)
(C) (7, 16, 9)
(D) (6, 14, 11)
Answer: (D) (6, 14, 11)
Solution: Compare the sum of each set.
A: 8 + 15 + 9 = 32
B: 5 + 14 + 13 = 32
C: 7 + 16 + 9 = 32
D: 6 + 14 + 11 = 31
Therefore, D is the odd set because its total is 31, while the other three totals are 32.

📌 Practice Strategy

Classification questions should be solved by identifying the exact common property shared by three options and then locating the option that does not follow that property.

For SSC-level preparation, pay special attention to number relations, alphabet positions, mathematical patterns and three-number set relations.
