📘 SSC Reasoning – Series

Complete concept-based preparation for Series questions with important types, patterns, methods, examples, shortcuts and SSC-level practice.

📚 What is Series?

A Series is a sequence of numbers, letters or symbols arranged according to a particular pattern or rule.

In SSC Reasoning, Series questions mainly test your ability to identify the hidden relationship between consecutive elements and find the missing or next element.

Series = Identify the Pattern → Apply the Rule → Find the Answer

🎯 Basic Concept of Series

Consider the following number series:

2, 4, 6, 8, 10, ?

Here every number increases by 2.

Therefore:

10 + 2 = 12

Answer = 12

📖 Types of Series Questions

1. Number Series

Series based on numbers and their mathematical relationships.

2. Alphabet Series

Series based on alphabet positions, forward movement, backward movement and letter gaps.

3. Alphanumeric Series

Series containing a combination of alphabets, numbers and sometimes symbols.

4. Mixed Series

Series involving more than one type of pattern or operation.

5. Missing Number Series

A number is missing from the sequence and must be identified using the pattern.

6. Wrong Number Series

One element does not follow the established pattern and must be identified.

🔢 Number Series

Number Series questions are based on mathematical relationships between consecutive numbers.

1. Addition-Based Series

5, 10, 15, 20, 25, ?

Pattern: +5

Answer = 30

2. Subtraction-Based Series

50, 45, 40, 35, 30, ?

Pattern: −5

Answer = 25

3. Multiplication-Based Series

2, 4, 8, 16, 32, ?

Pattern: ×2

Answer = 64

4. Division-Based Series

128, 64, 32, 16, 8, ?

Pattern: ÷2

Answer = 4

📊 Difference-Based Series

Sometimes the difference between consecutive numbers follows a pattern.

2, 5, 10, 17, 26, ?

Differences are:

+3, +5, +7, +9

The differences increase by 2.

Next difference = +11

Therefore:

26 + 11 = 37

Answer = 37

🔷 Square and Cube-Based Series

Square Series

1, 4, 9, 16, 25, ?

These are: 1², 2², 3², 4², 5²

Therefore next = 6² = 36

Cube Series

1, 8, 27, 64, ?

These are: 1³, 2³, 3³, 4³

Therefore next = 5³ = 125

🔤 Alphabet Series

Alphabet Series questions use the positions of letters in the English alphabet.

A = 1, B = 2, C = 3, ... Z = 26

Example 1

A, C, E, G, I, ?

Pattern: Every alternate letter.

Answer = K

Example 2

Z, X, V, T, R, ?

Pattern: Move backward by 2 letters.

Answer = P

🔠 Alphabet Position-Based Series

Convert letters into their numerical positions whenever the pattern is not immediately visible.

Example:

B, E, H, K, ?

Positions: 2, 5, 8, 11

Difference = +3

Next position = 14

Answer = N

🔢🔤 Alphanumeric Series

Alphanumeric Series combines alphabets and numbers in a single sequence.

A 2 B 4 C 6 D 8 E ?

Alphabet sequence: A, B, C, D, E

Number sequence: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10

Answer = 10

🔄 Mixed Series

In mixed series, more than one mathematical operation may be used.

2, 5, 11, 23, 47, ?

Pattern:

×2 +1

2 × 2 + 1 = 5

5 × 2 + 1 = 11

11 × 2 + 1 = 23

23 × 2 + 1 = 47

Therefore:

47 × 2 + 1 = 95

Answer = 95

❓ Missing Number Series

In these questions, one number is missing from the series. Identify the pattern and find the missing value.

3, 6, 12, ?, 48

Pattern: ×2

12 × 2 = 24

Therefore: Answer = 24

⚠️ Wrong Number Series

In a wrong number series, one number violates the established pattern.

2, 4, 8, 16, 31, 64

Expected pattern: ×2

2 × 2 = 4

4 × 2 = 8

8 × 2 = 16

16 × 2 = 32

Therefore 31 is the wrong number.

🧠 How to Solve Series Questions

Step 1 – Observe the Series

Read all the given numbers or letters carefully.

Step 2 – Check Simple Operations

First check addition, subtraction, multiplication and division.

Step 3 – Check Differences

If a simple operation does not work, calculate the differences between consecutive terms.

Step 4 – Check Special Patterns

Look for squares, cubes, prime numbers, alternate operations and increasing or decreasing differences.

Step 5 – Verify

Apply the identified rule to all terms to confirm that the pattern is consistent.

Observe → Find Pattern → Apply Rule → Verify → Answer
