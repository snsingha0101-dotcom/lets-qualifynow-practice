📘 SSC Reasoning – Analogy

Complete concept-based preparation for Analogy questions with types, methods, examples and SSC-level practice.

🎯 What is Analogy?

Analogy is a type of reasoning question in which a relationship between two elements is given and the same relationship has to be identified in another pair.

In simple words, Analogy means finding a similar relationship.

📚 Basic Concept

An analogy question generally follows the pattern:

A : B :: C : ?

Here, the relationship between A and B has to be applied to C to find the missing element.

Example

Book : Read :: Food : ?

A book is something we read. Similarly, food is something we eat.

Answer: Eat

🔹 Types of Analogy

1. Word Analogy

Relationship between two words is identified and applied to another word.

  • Object – Function
  • Person – Place
  • Worker – Workplace
  • Animal – Young One
  • Country – Capital

2. Number Analogy

A numerical relationship is identified and applied to another number.

  • Addition / Subtraction
  • Multiplication / Division
  • Square / Cube
  • Difference-based relation
  • Mixed operations

3. Alphabet Analogy

Relationships are established using alphabet positions.

  • Forward Position
  • Backward Position
  • Letter Gap
  • Position Difference
  • Letter Transformation

4. Figure Analogy

A relationship between figures is identified through transformation or movement.

  • Rotation
  • Reflection
  • Position Change
  • Shape Change
  • Pattern Transformation

🔤 Word Analogy

In Word Analogy, the relationship between two words may be based on their function, place, category, profession, object, person or another logical connection.

Example 1 – Object & Function

Pen : Write :: Knife : ?

A pen is used to write. A knife is used to cut.

Answer: Cut

Example 2 – Person & Workplace

Teacher : School :: Doctor : ?

A teacher works in a school. A doctor works in a hospital.

Answer: Hospital

Example 3 – Animal & Young One

Cow : Calf :: Horse : ?

The young one of a cow is called a calf. The young one of a horse is called a foal.

Answer: Foal

🔢 Number Analogy

Number Analogy questions are based on a mathematical relationship between two numbers.

Example 1

4 : 16 :: 7 : ?

Relationship: 4 × 4 = 16

Therefore: 7 × 7 = 49

Answer: 49

Example 2

5 : 15 :: 8 : ?

Relationship: 5 × 3 = 15

Therefore: 8 × 3 = 24

Answer: 24

Example 3

9 : 81 :: 6 : ?

Relationship: Number × Number

6 × 6 = 36

Answer: 36

🔠 Alphabet Analogy

Alphabet Analogy questions use the positions of letters in the English alphabet.

A = 1, B = 2, C = 3, ... Z = 26

Example 1

A : C :: D : ?

A से C तक +2 positions हैं। इसलिए D से +2 positions:

D → E → F

Answer: F

Example 2

B : E :: F : ?

B → E = +3

F → I = +3

Answer: I

🔷 Figure Analogy

Figure Analogy में दो figures के बीच transformation को पहचानकर उसी transformation को दूसरी figure पर लागू किया जाता है।

Rotation

Figure को clockwise या anticlockwise direction में rotate किया जा सकता है।

Reflection

Figure का mirror या reflection transformation दिया जा सकता है।

Position Change

Figure के अंदर मौजूद elements की position बदल सकती है।

Shape Transformation

Figure के आकार या pattern में logical परिवर्तन किया जा सकता है।

🧠 How to Solve Analogy Questions

1

Observe

पहले दिए गए pair को ध्यान से देखें।

2

Find Relation

दोनों elements के बीच exact relationship खोजें।

3

Apply

वही relationship दूसरे element पर लागू करें।

4

Verify

Options में relationship को दोबारा verify करें।

⭐ Important Points for SSC

  • सबसे पहले relationship identify करें।
  • केवल surface similarity देखकर answer select न करें।
  • Number Analogy में square, cube, multiplication, division, addition और subtraction जैसे patterns check करें।
  • Alphabet Analogy में letter positions का उपयोग करें।
  • Figure Analogy में rotation, reflection और position change को ध्यान से देखें।
  • अगर एक relationship से answer नहीं मिल रहा है, तो alternative logical relationship check करें।
  • SSC level पर speed के लिए common relationships की नियमित practice करें।
