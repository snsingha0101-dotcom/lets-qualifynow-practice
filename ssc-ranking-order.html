📘 SSC Reasoning – Ranking & Order

Complete concept-based preparation for Ranking and Order questions with rank from top, rank from bottom, total persons, position-based methods, examples and SSC-level practice.

📚 What is Ranking & Order?

Ranking and Order is an important Reasoning topic in which the position of a person or object is determined from the beginning, end, top, bottom, left or right.

Questions generally provide one or more positions and ask for the rank, total number of persons, position from the opposite side or number of persons between two positions.

Ranking = Identify Position → Understand Direction → Apply Formula → Find the Answer

🎯 Basic Concept

Ranking questions become easy when you clearly understand whether the position is counted from the beginning or from the opposite end.

⬆️ Rank from Top

Position counted from the top or beginning.

⬇️ Rank from Bottom

Position counted from the bottom or end.

⬅️ Rank from Left

Position counted from the left side.

➡️ Rank from Right

Position counted from the right side.

1️⃣ Rank from Top

If a person's position is counted from the top, the rank represents the number of people above that person plus one.

Rank from Top = Number of Persons Above + 1
Example:

Rahul is 7th from the top.

Therefore, 6 persons are above Rahul.

Rahul's Rank from Top = 7

2️⃣ Rank from Bottom

If a person's position is counted from the bottom, the rank represents the number of people below that person plus one.

Rank from Bottom = Number of Persons Below + 1
Example:

Sita is 5th from the bottom.

Therefore, 4 persons are below Sita.

Sita's Rank from Bottom = 5

🔢 Finding Total Number of Persons

When the rank of the same person is given from both ends, the total number of persons can be calculated directly.

Total Persons = Rank from Top + Rank from Bottom − 1
Example:

Rahul is 8th from the top and 13th from the bottom.

Total persons:

8 + 13 − 1 = 20

Therefore, there are 20 persons in total.

🔄 Finding Rank from the Opposite Side

If the total number of persons and rank from one side are known, rank from the opposite side can be found easily.

Opposite Rank = Total Persons − Given Rank + 1
Example:

There are 30 students in a class. Amit is 9th from the top.

Rank from bottom:

30 − 9 + 1 = 22

Amit is 22nd from the bottom.

👥 Number of Persons Between Two Positions

When the positions of two persons are given from the same side, the number of persons between them is obtained by subtracting the smaller position from the larger position and then subtracting one.

Persons Between = Difference of Ranks − 1
Example:

Rahul is 8th from the top and Amit is 15th from the top.

15 − 8 − 1 = 6

Therefore, 6 persons are between Rahul and Amit.

↕️ Positions from the Same Side

When two positions are measured from the same side, the difference between their ranks helps determine the number of people between them.

Example:

A student is 12th from the left and another student is 19th from the left.

Persons Between = 19 − 12 − 1 = 6

↔️ Positions from Opposite Sides

When positions are given from opposite sides, first determine the total number of persons or use the appropriate relationship between the positions.

Example:

Rahul is 7th from the left and 9th from the right.

Total persons:

7 + 9 − 1 = 15

Therefore, there are 15 persons in the row.

🔁 Position After Interchange

In some questions, two persons exchange their positions. The new position of each person becomes the previous position of the other person.

Example:

A is 6th from the top and B is 15th from the top. They interchange their positions.

After interchange:

A becomes 15th from the top and B becomes 6th from the top.

↔️ Left and Right Ranking

The same ranking formulas can be applied to positions counted from the left and right sides of a row.

Total = Left Position + Right Position − 1
Example:

A person is 11th from the left and 14th from the right.

Total Persons = 11 + 14 − 1 = 24

📊 Order-Based Questions

Some questions provide a sequence or arrangement and ask you to identify the position of a particular person.

Example:

In a row of students, Rahul is taller than five students but shorter than three students.

Therefore, three students are above Rahul in the given height order and five are below him.

🧠 How to Solve Ranking & Order Questions

Step 1 – Identify the Reference Side

Check whether the rank is from top, bottom, left or right.

Step 2 – Note the Given Rank

Write down the position clearly.

Step 3 – Identify Whether Both Sides Are Given

If ranks from both sides are given, use the total-person formula.

Step 4 – Apply the Correct Formula

Use the formula according to the type of question.

Step 5 – Check Whether One or Two Positions Are Involved

Questions involving two persons may ask for the number of persons between them.

Step 6 – Verify the Answer

Check whether the calculated rank is logically possible.

Identify Side → Note Rank → Apply Formula → Calculate → Verify

⭐ Important Ranking Formulas

Total Persons

Rank from Top + Rank from Bottom − 1


Opposite Rank

Total Persons − Given Rank + 1


Persons Between

Difference of Ranks − 1

⭐ Important Points for SSC

  • Always identify the side from which ranking is counted.
  • Remember the formula: Top + Bottom − 1.
  • For left and right positions, the same formula applies.
  • Persons between two positions = Difference − 1 when both positions are from the same side.
  • Do not confuse rank with the number of persons before or after a person.
  • In interchange questions, carefully note the original positions.
  • Check whether the question asks for rank, total persons or persons between two positions.
  • Read words such as top, bottom, left and right carefully.
  • Practice different ranking patterns for speed.

📝 SSC-Level Practice Questions

Q1. Rahul is 7th from the top and 13th from the bottom. How many students are there in the class?

A) 18    B) 19    C) 20    D) 21

Answer: B) 19
Q2. Amit is 9th from the top in a class of 30 students. What is his rank from the bottom?

A) 21    B) 22    C) 23    D) 24

Answer: B) 22
Q3. A student is 8th from the top and another student is 15th from the top. How many students are between them?

A) 5    B) 6    C) 7    D) 8

Answer: B) 6
Q4. A person is 11th from the left and 14th from the right. How many persons are there in the row?

A) 23    B) 24    C) 25    D) 26

Answer: B) 24
Q5. Sita is 5th from the bottom in a row of 18 students. What is her rank from the top?

A) 13    B) 14    C) 15    D) 16

Answer: B) 14
Q6. A is 6th from the top and B is 15th from the top. They exchange positions. What is A's new rank from the top?

A) 6    B) 9    C) 15    D) 21

Answer: C) 15
Q7. Rahul is 10th from the left and 12th from the right. How many persons are there in the row?

A) 20    B) 21    C) 22    D) 23

Answer: B) 21
Q8. A student is 12th from the top. There are 8 students below him. What is the total number of students?

A) 19    B) 20    C) 21    D) 22

Answer: B) 20

⚡ Quick Revision

  • Rank from top = position counted from the beginning.
  • Rank from bottom = position counted from the end.
  • Total Persons = Top Rank + Bottom Rank − 1.
  • Opposite Rank = Total − Given Rank + 1.
  • Persons Between = Difference of Ranks − 1.
  • The same principles apply to left and right positions.
  • In interchange questions, positions are exchanged.
  • Always identify exactly what the question is asking.
