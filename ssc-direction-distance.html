📚 What is Direction & Distance?

Direction and Distance is an important topic of Reasoning in which the position, movement and distance of a person or object are determined using different directions.

Questions may involve movement towards North, South, East or West along with turns, distances and final positions.

Direction & Distance = Understand Movement → Track Position → Calculate Distance → Find Direction

⬆️ North

Upward direction in a standard direction diagram.

⬇️ South

Opposite to North.

➡️ East

Right side when facing North.

⬅️ West

Left side when facing North.

🔄 Opposite Directions

North ↔ South

East ↔ West

North-East ↔ South-West

North-West ↔ South-East

Remembering opposite directions makes many questions faster to solve.

↗️ Intermediate Directions

When movement occurs between two primary directions, an intermediate direction may be formed.

North-East

Between North and East.

North-West

Between North and West.

South-East

Between South and East.

South-West

Between South and West.

↔️ Right and Left Turns

The direction of a right or left turn depends on the direction in which the person is currently facing.

Example:

A person is facing North. He turns right.

Right turn from North = East
Example:

A person is facing East. He turns left.

Left turn from East = North

🔄 Important Turn Table

Facing North → Right = East → Left = West

Facing East → Right = South → Left = North

Facing South → Right = West → Left = East

Facing West → Right = North → Left = South

📏 Distance Concept

Distance questions require us to identify the actual distance travelled or the shortest distance between two points.

Example:

A person walks 5 km North and then 5 km South.

Total distance travelled = 10 km.

Final distance from starting point = 0 km.

Total Distance ≠ Shortest Distance from Starting Point

📐 Shortest Distance Using Pythagoras

When movement occurs in perpendicular directions, the shortest distance can be calculated using the Pythagoras theorem.

Shortest Distance = √(North/South Distance² + East/West Distance²)
Example:

A person walks 3 km North and then 4 km East.

Shortest distance from starting point:

√(3² + 4²) = √25 = 5 km

🚶 Turning-Based Questions

In turning-based questions, carefully track the direction after every movement and turn.

Example:

A person is facing North. He walks forward and then turns right. He walks again and turns left.

Always update the current facing direction after every turn.

Never assume that every right turn means East. The answer depends on the current facing direction.
