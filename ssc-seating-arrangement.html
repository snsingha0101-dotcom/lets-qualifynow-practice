🪑 Seating Arrangement

Learn the concepts, directions, arrangement patterns and logical techniques required to solve SSC-level Seating Arrangement questions.

📘 What is Seating Arrangement?

Seating Arrangement is a reasoning topic in which several persons are placed in different positions according to given conditions.

You have to understand the given clues, determine the positions of the persons and answer questions based on the final arrangement.

Key Idea: First identify the arrangement type and the direction in which people are facing. Then place the strongest clues and complete the arrangement step by step.

🔹 Important Types of Seating Arrangement

1. Linear Arrangement

Persons are seated in a straight line, usually facing North or South.

2. Circular Arrangement

Persons are seated around a circle and may face the centre or outside.

3. Square Arrangement

Persons are seated around a square-shaped arrangement with positions at corners or sides.

4. Rectangular Arrangement

Persons are arranged around a rectangle according to different conditions.

5. Facing Inside / Outside

Persons may face the centre of the arrangement or face away from the centre.

6. Mixed Arrangement

Some questions combine seating conditions with additional information or multiple arrangements.

📝 Important Seating Terms

  • Immediate Left: The person sitting directly to the left.
  • Immediate Right: The person sitting directly to the right.
  • Immediate Neighbour: A person sitting directly next to another person.
  • Second to the Left: The person two positions to the left.
  • Second to the Right: The person two positions to the right.
  • Between: A person occupying a position between two specified persons.
  • Adjacent: Sitting directly next to another person.
  • Opposite: Sitting directly across from another person in a circular arrangement.
  • Extreme End: The first or last position in a linear arrangement.

🧭 Direction Rules in Linear Arrangement

Direction becomes extremely important when persons are facing different directions.

Facing North

Left means West and Right means East.

Facing South

Left means East and Right means West.

Important: Never assume left and right without checking the direction in which the person is facing.

🔄 Circular Arrangement Rules

When Everyone Faces the Centre

For a person facing the centre:

  • Left side → Clockwise direction
  • Right side → Anticlockwise direction

When Everyone Faces Outside

For a person facing outside:

  • Left side → Anticlockwise direction
  • Right side → Clockwise direction
Golden Rule: In circular arrangements, always determine the facing direction before applying left and right.

🧠 Basic Approach to Seating Arrangement

  1. Identify the type of arrangement.
  2. Check the direction in which people are facing.
  3. Count the total number of positions.
  4. Identify the strongest or fixed clue.
  5. Place the fixed person or pair first.
  6. Connect immediate-neighbour clues.
  7. Use left/right and opposite-position clues.
  8. Check all conditions before answering.
Golden Rule: Do not solve a seating arrangement only in your mind. Draw the positions clearly and fill them step by step.

📊 Solving a Linear Arrangement

Five persons A, B, C, D and E are sitting in a row facing North.

A sits at the extreme left.
B sits immediately to the right of A.
C sits immediately to the right of B.

The arrangement begins as:

A → B → C → ? → ?

The remaining persons can then be placed using the remaining clues.

Learning Point: In a linear arrangement, start with extreme positions and immediate-neighbour conditions whenever they are available.

🔵 Solved Example 1 — Linear Seating

Five persons A, B, C, D and E sit in a row facing North.

A sits at the extreme left.
B sits immediately to the right of A.
C sits immediately to the right of B.
D sits at the extreme right.

Therefore:

A → B → C → E → D

Question: Who sits between C and D?

Answer: E
E sits between C and D.

🔵 Solved Example 2 — Facing South

Four persons P, Q, R and S sit in a row facing South.

Q sits immediately to the left of R.
P sits immediately to the left of Q.
S sits at the remaining position.

Since everyone is facing South, their left is our right.

S → R → Q → P
Learning Point: When people face South, their left and right directions are opposite to our visual left and right.

🔵 Solved Example 3 — Circular Arrangement

Four persons A, B, C and D sit around a circle facing the centre.

A sits opposite C.
B sits opposite D.

One possible representation is:

A ↔ C     B ↔ D

The exact starting position in a circular arrangement is generally not important; relative positions are important.

Learning Point: In circular arrangements, focus on relative positions such as opposite, immediate neighbour and left/right.

🔵 Solved Example 4 — Immediate Neighbours

Six persons P, Q, R, S, T and U sit in a row.

P sits immediately before Q.
R sits immediately after Q.

Therefore, the three-person block is:

P → Q → R

This block should be treated as a single unit while solving the remaining conditions.

Learning Point: Whenever two or more people have immediate-position relations, create a block first.

🔍 Immediate Neighbour & Second Position

Immediate Neighbour

If A sits immediately to the right of B, there is no person between A and B.

Example: B → A

Second Position

If A sits second to the right of B, exactly one position lies between them.

Example: B → X → A

⚠️ Common Direction Mistakes

  • Forgetting whether the person is facing North or South.
  • Applying the same left/right rule to every person.
  • Confusing our left with the person's left.
  • Ignoring centre/outside direction in circular arrangements.
  • Assuming an arrangement without checking all clues.
Exam Tip: Always write the facing direction before starting a direction-based seating question.

🎯 How to Solve Seating Arrangement Questions

  1. Identify whether the arrangement is linear or circular.
  2. Check the facing direction.
  3. Draw the required number of positions.
  4. Place fixed positions first.
  5. Make immediate-neighbour blocks.
  6. Apply left/right conditions carefully.
  7. Use opposite and second-position clues.
  8. Eliminate impossible arrangements.
  9. Verify every clue.
  10. Answer only after the complete arrangement is confirmed.

📝 SSC-Level Practice Questions

Q1.

Five persons A, B, C, D and E sit in a row facing North. A sits at the extreme left. B sits immediately to the right of A. C sits immediately to the right of B. D sits at the extreme right. Who sits between C and D?

Answer: E

Q2.

Six persons P, Q, R, S, T and U sit in a row facing North. P sits at the extreme left. Q sits immediately to the right of P. R sits immediately to the right of Q. U sits at the extreme right. Who sits between R and U?

Answer: S and T

Q3.

Four persons A, B, C and D sit in a row facing South. B sits immediately to the left of C. A sits immediately to the left of B. Who sits immediately to the right of C from the person's perspective?

Answer: D

Q4.

Five persons P, Q, R, S and T sit in a row facing North. Q sits immediately between P and R. S sits at the extreme right. If P is at the second position, who sits at the fourth position?

Answer: R

Q5.

Seven persons A, B, C, D, E, F and G sit in a row facing North. D sits at the centre. B sits immediately to the left of D. F sits immediately to the right of D. A sits at the extreme left and G at the extreme right. Who sits immediately to the left of F?

Answer: D

Q6.

Six persons A, B, C, D, E and F sit around a circle facing the centre. A sits opposite D. B sits opposite E. Who sits opposite C?

Answer: F

Q7.

Five persons P, Q, R, S and T sit in a row facing North. P sits immediately before Q. R sits immediately after Q. S sits at the extreme left. Who sits immediately before R?

Answer: Q

Q8.

Eight persons A, B, C, D, E, F, G and H sit in a row facing North. A sits at the extreme left and H at the extreme right. B sits immediately to the right of A. G sits immediately to the left of H. C sits immediately to the right of B. Who sits between C and G?

Answer: D, E and F

❌ Common Mistakes

  • Not checking the facing direction.
  • Confusing immediate neighbour with second position.
  • Using left/right rules incorrectly.
  • Forgetting whether people face the centre or outside.
  • Starting from a weak clue instead of a fixed clue.
  • Not creating blocks for immediate relations.
  • Ignoring one of the given conditions.
  • Not verifying the final arrangement.

⚡ Quick Solving Method

  1. Identify the arrangement type.
  2. Check the facing direction.
  3. Draw the positions.
  4. Place fixed clues.
  5. Create immediate-neighbour blocks.
  6. Connect the remaining clues.
  7. Eliminate impossible cases.
  8. Verify the complete arrangement.
  9. Answer the question.
Exam Tip: Seating Arrangement becomes much easier when the positions are drawn clearly instead of being solved mentally.

📌 Quick Revision

  • Identify the arrangement type first.
  • Check whether people face North, South, centre or outside.
  • Use fixed positions first.
  • Make blocks for immediate-neighbour conditions.
  • Remember that left/right depends on facing direction.
  • In circular arrangements, relative position is important.
  • Use opposite-position clues carefully.
  • Verify every condition before answering.
  • Practice improves speed and accuracy.
