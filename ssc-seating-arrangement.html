🪑 Seating Arrangement

Learn the concepts, directions, arrangement patterns and logical techniques required to solve SSC-level Seating Arrangement questions.

📘 What is Seating Arrangement?

Seating Arrangement is a reasoning topic in which several persons are placed in different positions according to given conditions.

You have to understand the given clues, determine the positions of the persons and answer questions based on the final arrangement.

Key Idea: First identify the arrangement type and the direction in which people are facing. Then place the strongest clues and complete the arrangement step by step.

🔹 Important Types of Seating Arrangement

1. Linear Arrangement

Persons are seated in a straight line, usually facing North or South.

2. Circular Arrangement

Persons are seated around a circle and may face the centre or outside.

3. Square Arrangement

Persons are seated around a square-shaped arrangement with positions at corners or sides.

4. Rectangular Arrangement

Persons are arranged around a rectangle according to different conditions.

5. Facing Inside / Outside

Persons may face the centre of the arrangement or face away from the centre.

6. Mixed Arrangement

Some questions combine seating conditions with additional information or multiple arrangements.

📝 Important Seating Terms

  • Immediate Left: The person sitting directly to the left.
  • Immediate Right: The person sitting directly to the right.
  • Immediate Neighbour: A person sitting directly next to another person.
  • Second to the Left: The person two positions to the left.
  • Second to the Right: The person two positions to the right.
  • Between: A person occupying a position between two specified persons.
  • Adjacent: Sitting directly next to another person.
  • Opposite: Sitting directly across from another person in a circular arrangement.
  • Extreme End: The first or last position in a linear arrangement.

🧭 Direction Rules in Linear Arrangement

Direction becomes extremely important when persons are facing different directions.

Facing North

Left means West and Right means East.

Facing South

Left means East and Right means West.

Important: Never assume left and right without checking the direction in which the person is facing.

🔄 Circular Arrangement Rules

When Everyone Faces the Centre

For a person facing the centre:

  • Left side → Clockwise direction
  • Right side → Anticlockwise direction

When Everyone Faces Outside

For a person facing outside:

  • Left side → Anticlockwise direction
  • Right side → Clockwise direction
Golden Rule: In circular arrangements, always determine the facing direction before applying left and right.

🧠 Basic Approach to Seating Arrangement

  1. Identify the type of arrangement.
  2. Check the direction in which people are facing.
  3. Count the total number of positions.
  4. Identify the strongest or fixed clue.
  5. Place the fixed person or pair first.
  6. Connect immediate-neighbour clues.
  7. Use left/right and opposite-position clues.
  8. Check all conditions before answering.
Golden Rule: Do not solve a seating arrangement only in your mind. Draw the positions clearly and fill them step by step.

📊 Solving a Linear Arrangement

Five persons A, B, C, D and E are sitting in a row facing North.

A sits at the extreme left.
B sits immediately to the right of A.
C sits immediately to the right of B.

The arrangement begins as:

A → B → C → ? → ?

The remaining persons can then be placed using the remaining clues.

Learning Point: In a linear arrangement, start with extreme positions and immediate-neighbour conditions whenever they are available.
