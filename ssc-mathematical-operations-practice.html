📌 Important Mathematical Operations Rules

  • Always follow BODMAS wherever applicable.
  • In sign-interchange questions, first replace all the signs according to the given condition.
  • After replacing the signs, solve the expression carefully.
  • Multiplication and division are performed before addition and subtraction when no brackets change the order.
  • For defined-operation questions, first identify the rule from the given example and then apply the same rule to the required values.
