📘 What is Word Formation?

Word Formation questions are based on the arrangement, selection and comparison of letters of a given word.

In SSC Reasoning, these questions test your understanding of alphabet positions, letter arrangement and the formation of meaningful or specified words.

🎯 SSC Focus: Carefully observe the given word, identify the required letters, compare their positions and follow the exact condition given in the question.

📊 Important Types of Word Formation Questions

1️⃣ Word Formation from Given Letters

You may be asked whether a particular word can be formed using the letters of a given word.

2️⃣ Meaningful Word Formation

Questions may ask you to arrange selected letters to form a meaningful English word.

3️⃣ Letter Selection

You may need to identify letters occupying particular positions in the given word.

4️⃣ Position-Based Questions

Questions can ask which letter is third from the left, fourth from the right, etc.

5️⃣ Alphabetical Arrangement

Letters of a word may need to be arranged in alphabetical order and their new positions may be asked.

🧠 Method to Check Word Formation

  1. Write the given word clearly.
  2. Count the frequency of every required letter.
  3. Compare the letters required in the target word.
  4. Check whether every required letter is available.
  5. Check whether any letter is required more times than available.
  6. Give the final answer according to the question.
⚡ Important: A word can be formed only when every required letter is available in sufficient quantity.

📝 Solved Examples

Question 1: Can the word RAIN be formed from the letters of REASONING?
Solution:
R, A, I and N are all present in REASONING.

Answer: Yes
Question 2: Can the word TRAIN be formed from REASONING?
T is not present in REASONING.

Answer: No
Question 3: Can the word RING be formed from REASONING?
R, I, N and G are available.

Answer: Yes
Question 4: Which letter is 4th from the left in the word REASONING?
R – E – A – S

Answer: S
Question 5: Which letter is 3rd from the right in the word REASONING?
From right: G – N – I

Answer: I

🔤 Alphabetical Arrangement of Letters

Sometimes all letters of a word are rearranged in alphabetical order.

Example:
BRAIN
Alphabetical order:
A B I N R
After rearrangement, questions may ask for the position of a particular letter.

⬅️ Left and Right Position

Always identify whether the question asks for the position from the left or from the right.

Example:
C O M P E T I T I V E
If a question asks for the 5th letter from the left:
C – O – M – P – E

Answer: E

🔢 Odd and Even Positions

In some questions, letters at odd or even positions may be considered separately.

Word:
REASONING
Odd positions: 1, 3, 5, 7, 9
Letters: R, A, O, I, G

Even positions: 2, 4, 6, 8
Letters: E, S, N, N

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Ignoring repeated letters.
  • Counting positions incorrectly.
  • Confusing left position with right position.
  • Assuming a word can be formed without checking letter frequency.
  • Ignoring alphabetical order in arrangement questions.
  • Reading the word incorrectly due to similar-looking letters.
