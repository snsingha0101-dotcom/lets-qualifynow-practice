📘 SSC Reasoning – Classification

Complete concept-based preparation for Classification questions with methods, examples, shortcuts and SSC-level practice.

📚 What is Classification?

Classification is a type of Reasoning question in which a group of words, numbers, letters or figures is given. Among them, one item is different from the remaining items according to a particular logical relationship.

The main objective is to identify the item that does not belong to the same group.

Classification = Identify the Odd / Different Item

🎯 Basic Concept

In Classification questions, first identify the common relationship between most of the given items. The item that does not follow that relationship is generally the correct answer.

Example

Apple, Mango, Banana, Carrot

Apple, Mango and Banana are fruits, whereas Carrot is a vegetable.

Answer: Carrot

📖 Types of Classification

1. Word Classification

Questions are based on the meaning, category, function or relationship of words.

  • Person and Profession
  • Animal and Young One
  • Object and Function
  • Place and Associated Object
  • Category-based Classification

2. Number Classification

Questions are based on numerical properties and mathematical relationships.

  • Odd and Even Numbers
  • Prime and Composite Numbers
  • Square and Cube Numbers
  • Divisibility
  • Number Pattern

3. Alphabet Classification

Questions are based on alphabet positions, letter patterns and relationships.

  • Alphabet Position
  • Letter Gap
  • Forward and Backward Position
  • Vowels and Consonants
  • Letter Pattern

4. Figure Classification

Questions are based on visual patterns and relationships between figures.

  • Shape Pattern
  • Rotation
  • Reflection
  • Number of Elements
  • Position and Direction

🔤 Word Classification

In Word Classification, identify the common category or relationship among the words.

Q. Find the odd one out:

Doctor, Teacher, Engineer, Hospital

Answer: Hospital

Doctor, Teacher and Engineer are professions, while Hospital is a place.

Q. Find the odd one out:

Cow, Goat, Horse, Lion

Answer: Lion

Cow, Goat and Horse are generally domesticated animals, while Lion is a wild animal.

🔢 Number Classification

Number Classification requires identifying a number that does not follow the common numerical property.

Q. Find the odd number:

4, 9, 16, 25, 30

Answer: 30

4, 9, 16 and 25 are perfect squares. 30 is not a perfect square.

Q. Find the odd number:

3, 5, 7, 11, 15

Answer: 15

3, 5, 7 and 11 are prime numbers. 15 is a composite number.

🔡 Alphabet Classification

Alphabet Classification questions are solved by checking letter positions, gaps and patterns.

A = 1, B = 2, C = 3, ... Z = 26
Q. Find the odd one out:

A, E, I, O, B

Answer: B

A, E, I and O are vowels, while B is a consonant.

Q. Find the odd one out:

C, F, I, L, M

Answer: M

C, F, I and L follow a gap of three alphabet positions. M does not follow the pattern.

🔷 Figure Classification

Figure Classification questions are based on visual characteristics. Carefully observe each figure before deciding the common pattern.

Important Points

  • Check the number of sides.
  • Check the number of elements.
  • Observe rotation and direction.
  • Check reflection or mirror relationship.
  • Observe the position of dots, lines or arrows.
  • Compare the overall pattern of the figures.

🧠 How to Solve Classification Questions

Step 1 – Observe

Look at all the given words, numbers, letters or figures carefully.

Step 2 – Find the Common Property

Identify the relationship followed by most of the items.

Step 3 – Identify the Different Item

Find the item which does not follow the identified rule.

Step 4 – Verify

Check the remaining items once again to confirm that the selected option is genuinely different.

Observe → Find Common Property → Identify Odd Item → Verify

⭐ Important Points for SSC

  • Do not select an answer only because it looks different.
  • Always search for a logical relationship.
  • In number classification, check squares, cubes, prime numbers, divisibility and differences.
  • In alphabet classification, check alphabet positions and letter gaps.
  • In word classification, focus on meaning and category.
  • In figure classification, carefully observe rotation, reflection, position and number of elements.
  • Practice different patterns because SSC can use multiple approaches.
