🔹 Syllogism Reasoning

Complete concept-based preparation covering All, Some, No, Some Not, Possibility, Either-Or and Coded Syllogism.

📘 What is Syllogism?

Understand the basic logical relationship between different groups.

Syllogism is a logical reasoning topic in which one or more statements are given and we have to determine whether the given conclusion logically follows from those statements.
Example:

All Teachers are Educated.
All Educated Persons are Knowledgeable.

Conclusion:
All Teachers are Knowledgeable.

Result: Follows.

1️⃣ All Statement

All A are B

This means every member of A belongs to B.

Example:

All Doctors are Professionals.

Meaning:
Doctors → Professionals
⚠️ Important:
"All A are B" does NOT mean "All B are A".

2️⃣ Some Statement

Some A are B

At least one member of A is also a member of B.

Example:

Some Students are Athletes.

Meaning:
At least one Student is an Athlete.
✅ The overlapping portion between the two groups exists.

3️⃣ No Statement

No A are B

A and B have no common member.

Example:

No Cats are Birds.

Meaning:
Cats and Birds do not overlap.

4️⃣ Some Not Statement

Some A are Not B

At least one member of A does not belong to B.

Example:

Some Players are not Coaches.

Meaning:
At least one Player is outside the group of Coaches.
💡 "Some A are not B" is NOT the same as "Some B are not A".

🔵 Venn Diagram Approach

Venn diagrams make Syllogism easier by visually representing the relationship between different groups.

All A are B

A is completely inside B.
Some A are B

A and B have an overlapping portion.
No A are B

A and B remain completely separate.
Some A are Not B

At least one member of A lies outside B.

🔗 Chain of Statements

When one group is completely included in another group, the relationship can sometimes be carried forward.

Statements:

All Pens are Stationery.
All Stationery are Useful.

Conclusion:
All Pens are Useful.

Result: Follows.
✅ If A is inside B and B is inside C, then A is inside C.

❓ Possibility

Possibility questions ask whether a particular relationship can exist without contradicting the given statements.

Statement:

All Engineers are Graduates.

Possible conclusion:

Some Graduates may be Engineers.

Result: Possible.
💡 For possibility, always check whether the proposed relationship creates a contradiction.

↔️ Either-Or

Either-Or questions require careful examination of two conclusions which represent alternative possibilities.

Example:

Statements:
Some Artists are Writers.

Conclusions:
I. Some Artists are not Writers.
II. All Artists are Writers.
⚠️ Do not apply Either-Or merely because two conclusions look opposite. The complete logical conditions must be checked.

🔐 Coded Syllogism – Type 1

Exam-oriented coded statements using * % > < and the possibility symbol ?.

📌 Symbol Meaning

Symbol Meaning Example
* All A are B A * B
% Some A are B A % B
< No A are B A < B
> Some A are not B A > B

❓ Possibility Coding

When ? is attached to a coded relationship, the question asks whether that relationship is possible.

A * ? B
All A being B is possible.

A % ? B
Some A being B is possible.

A < ? B
No A being B is possible.

A > ? B
Some A being not B is possible.

🧠 Original Example 1

Coded statements:

Teachers * Mentors
Mentors * Guides

Conclusion:
Teachers * Guides

Answer: Follows.

Reason:
All Teachers are Mentors and all Mentors are Guides. Therefore all Teachers are Guides.

🧠 Original Example 2

Coded statement:

Artists % Designers

Meaning:
Some Artists are Designers.

Therefore the same overlapping members can also be described as some Designers being Artists.

🧠 Original Example 3

Coded statement:

Writers > Editors

Meaning:
Some Writers are not Editors.

🧠 Original Example 4

Coded statement:

Pilots < Sailors

Meaning:
No Pilots are Sailors.

🔐 Coded Syllogism – Type 2

Another commonly used coding pattern uses #, @, $ and ©.

📌 Symbol Meaning

Symbol Meaning Example
# All P are Q P # Q
@ Some P are Q P @ Q
© No P are Q P © Q
$ Some P are not Q P $ Q

🧠 Original Example 1

Coded statements:

Readers # Learners
Learners # Students

Conclusion:
Readers # Students

Answer: Follows.

🧠 Original Example 2

Coded statement:

Musicians @ Teachers

Meaning:
Some Musicians are Teachers.

🧠 Original Example 3

Coded statement:

Pilots © Drivers

Meaning:
No Pilots are Drivers.

🧠 Original Example 4

Coded statement:

Players $ Coaches

Meaning:
Some Players are not Coaches.

🧩 How to Solve Coded Syllogism

  1. First identify the symbol between the two words.
  2. Convert the symbol into its actual logical statement.
  3. Arrange the relationships mentally or through a Venn Diagram.
  4. Check whether the conclusion directly follows.
  5. For possibility questions, check whether the proposed relationship creates a contradiction.
  6. Pay special attention to "Some Not" because it cannot simply be reversed.

📝 Practice Questions

Original practice questions based on the coded Syllogism pattern.

Question 1


Statements:

Teachers * Mentors
Mentors * Guides

Conclusion:

I. Teachers * Guides

Answer: Follows
All Teachers are Mentors and all Mentors are Guides.
Question 2


Statement:

Writers > Editors

Conclusion:

I. Some Writers are not Editors.

Answer: Follows
The symbol > represents Some A are not B.
Question 3


Statement:

Pilots < Sailors

Conclusion:

I. Some Pilots are Sailors.

Answer: Does not follow
The statement says that Pilots and Sailors have no common member.
Question 4


Statements:

Engineers * Graduates
Engineers % Researchers

Conclusion:

I. Some Graduates are Researchers.

Answer: Follows
The Engineers who are Researchers are also Graduates.
Question 5


Statements:

Readers # Learners
Learners # Students

Conclusion:

I. Readers # Students

Answer: Follows
The relationship forms a complete chain.
Question 6


Statement:

Musicians @ Teachers

Conclusion:

I. Some Teachers are Musicians.

Answer: Follows
The overlapping members are both Musicians and Teachers.
Question 7


Statement:

Pilots © Drivers

Conclusion:

I. Some Pilots are Drivers.

Answer: Does not follow
The symbol © means No P are Q.
Question 8


Statement:

Players $ Coaches

Conclusion:

I. Some Players are not Coaches.

Answer: Follows
The symbol $ represents Some P are not Q.
Question 9


Statements:

Doctors # Professionals.
Some Professionals are Researchers.

Conclusion:

I. Some Doctors are Researchers.

Answer: Cannot be established
All Doctors are Professionals, but the given information does not establish that the Researchers are Doctors.
Question 10


Statements:

Teachers * Educators
Teachers % Researchers

Conclusion:

I. Some Educators are Researchers.

Answer: Follows
The Teachers who are Researchers are also Educators.

🎯 Important Points for SSC

  • ✔️ All A are B does not mean All B are A.
  • ✔️ Some A are B establishes an overlap.
  • ✔️ No A are B means there is no overlap.
  • ✔️ Some A are Not B means at least one A lies outside B.
  • ✔️ Possibility must not contradict any statement.
  • ✔️ Decode coded symbols before solving the question.
  • ✔️ "Some Not" is not reversible.
  • ✔️ Chain relationships are important.
  • ✔️ Never assume information which is not given.
