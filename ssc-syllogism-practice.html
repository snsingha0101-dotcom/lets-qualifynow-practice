Important Syllogism Rules
Syllogism के महत्वपूर्ण नियम

  • All A are B means A is completely contained within B.
    All A are B का अर्थ है कि A के सभी तत्व B के अंतर्गत आते हैं।
  • Under the SSC-style existence convention used in this practice set, All A are B also gives Some A are B and Some B are A.
    इस अभ्यास सेट में प्रयुक्त SSC-शैली की अस्तित्व मान्यता के अनुसार, All A are B से Some A are B तथा Some B are A भी माना जाता है।
  • Some A are B means there is an overlap between A and B.
    Some A are B का अर्थ है कि A और B में कुछ तत्व समान हैं।
  • Some A are B is reversible: Some B are A.
    Some A are B को उलटा भी लिखा जा सकता है: Some B are A.
  • No A is B means A and B cannot overlap.
    No A is B का अर्थ है कि A और B में कोई समान तत्व नहीं है।
  • No A is B is reversible: No B is A.
    No A is B को उलटकर No B is A भी लिखा जा सकता है।
  • Under the SSC-style existence convention, No A is B can also support Some A are not B when the existence of A is relevant.
    SSC-शैली की अस्तित्व मान्यता के अनुसार, जब A के अस्तित्व को माना जाता है, तब No A is B से Some A are not B भी समर्थित हो सकता है।
  • Some A are not B means at least one A exists outside B.
    Some A are not B का अर्थ है कि कम-से-कम एक A ऐसा है जो B के अंतर्गत नहीं आता।
  • Only A are B means All B are A.
    Only A are B का अर्थ है All B are A।
  • Only a few A are B means Some A are B and Some A are not B.
    Only a few A are B का अर्थ है Some A are B तथा Some A are not B।
  • A Few A are B means Some A are B; it does not mean All A are B.
    A Few A are B का अर्थ है Some A are B; इसका अर्थ यह नहीं है कि All A are B।
  • All A are B is not reversible as All B are A. Its valid particular conversion is Some B are A.
    All A are B को उलटकर All B are A नहीं किया जा सकता। इसका मान्य particular रूप Some B are A है।
  • Possibility conclusions must always be checked against all given statements.
    Possibility वाले निष्कर्षों को हमेशा दिए गए सभी कथनों के आधार पर जाँचना चाहिए।
  • A possibility conclusion is valid when at least one logically consistent arrangement satisfies it.
    कोई Possibility conclusion तब सही होता है जब कम-से-कम एक ऐसा तार्किक arrangement संभव हो जो दिए गए सभी कथनों के अनुरूप हो।
  • In possibility questions, do not assume any additional relationship between terms that is not supported by the statements.
    Possibility वाले प्रश्नों में ऐसे किसी अतिरिक्त संबंध को नहीं मानना चाहिए जिसका समर्थन दिए गए कथनों से नहीं होता है।
