📘 What are Mathematical Operations?

Mathematical Operations questions in SSC Reasoning are based on mathematical symbols and the rules assigned to them.

In these questions, the usual mathematical symbols may be replaced by other symbols. You have to understand the given rule and then solve the expression correctly.

🎯 SSC Focus: First identify the meaning of each symbol, replace the symbols correctly and then apply the proper mathematical order of operations.

🔄 Operator Replacement

In many SSC questions, the symbols are assigned different meanings.

Example:
If + means × and × means −
Then:
6 + 3 × 2
should be interpreted as:
6 × 3 − 2
⚠️ Never solve the original expression before replacing the symbols according to the question.

🧠 BODMAS Rule

After replacing the symbols, apply the correct mathematical order.

B → Brackets
O → Orders / Powers
D → Division
M → Multiplication
A → Addition
S → Subtraction
Important: If brackets are present, solve the bracketed expression first. Then follow the remaining mathematical order.

📝 SSC-Level Solved Examples

Question 1: If + means × and × means +, find:
4 + 3 × 2
Replace the symbols:

4 × 3 + 2

= 12 + 2

Answer = 14
Question 2: If − means ÷ and ÷ means −, find:
18 − 3 ÷ 2
Replace the symbols:

18 ÷ 3 − 2

= 6 − 2

Answer = 4
Question 3: If × means + and + means −, find:
12 × 5 + 3
Replace:

12 + 5 − 3

= 14

Answer = 14
Question 4: If ÷ means × and × means ÷, find:
20 ÷ 5 × 2
Replace:

20 × 5 ÷ 2

= 100 ÷ 2

Answer = 50
Question 5: If + means ÷, find:
24 + 6
24 ÷ 6 = 4

🔲 Operations with Brackets

When brackets are present, they should normally be solved before the other operations.

Example:
5 + (6 × 2)
First:
6 × 2 = 12

Then:
5 + 12 = 17

🔀 Mixed Operations

Some questions contain several replaced operators together. Solve them systematically.

Example: If:
+ means ×
× means ÷
÷ means +
Find:
12 + 6 × 3 ÷ 2
Replace:
12 × 6 ÷ 3 + 2
Apply the correct order:

12 × 6 = 72
72 ÷ 3 = 24
24 + 2 = 26

⚖️ Comparing Mathematical Expressions

Some questions ask you to compare two expressions using symbols such as >, < or =.

Example: Compare:
15 + 5    and    12 × 2
15 + 5 = 20
12 × 2 = 24

Therefore:
15 + 5 < 12 × 2

🧠 How to Solve Mathematical Operation Questions

  1. Read every symbol definition carefully.
  2. Write the original expression separately.
  3. Replace every symbol according to the question.
  4. Do not change the numbers.
  5. Apply BODMAS after replacement.
  6. Solve brackets first when present.
  7. Check the final answer once.
⚡ Fast Method: Symbol replacement → Correct expression → BODMAS → Final answer

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Forgetting to replace one of the symbols.
  • Applying BODMAS before replacing symbols.
  • Changing numbers instead of operators.
  • Ignoring brackets.
  • Solving from left to right without following mathematical priority.
  • Assuming that the normal meaning of a symbol still applies.
