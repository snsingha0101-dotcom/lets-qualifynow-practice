🔷 Figure Analogy

Understand the relationship between two figures and apply the same relationship to identify the correct corresponding figure.

📘 What is Figure Analogy?

Figure Analogy is a non-verbal reasoning topic in which two figures are related in a particular way. A third figure is then given and the student has to identify the figure that will have the same relationship with it.

Basic Pattern: Figure A : Figure B :: Figure C : ?

The relationship between A and B must be applied to C to find the correct answer.

🎯 Important Types of Figure Analogy

1. Shape Transformation A shape changes into another shape according to a fixed rule.
2. Rotation The complete figure may rotate by a fixed angle.
3. Addition of Element A line, dot, arrow or another element may be added.
4. Removal of Element One element may disappear according to the rule.
5. Position Change The internal element may move from one position to another.
6. Direction Change An arrow or pointed element may change its direction.
7. Size Transformation An inner or outer figure may become larger or smaller.
8. Combination Rule More than one visual transformation may occur together.

🧠 How to Solve Figure Analogy

1
Compare A and B

Find exactly what has changed from the first figure to the second.

2
Ignore Appearance

Focus on the relationship rather than the overall look of the figure.

3
Check Direction

Observe arrows, lines, triangles and pointed elements.

4
Check Position

Compare the relative positions of internal elements.

5
Apply the Rule

Use exactly the same transformation on Figure C.

6
Match the Option

Select the option that follows the same transformation.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Looking only at the outer shape.
  • Ignoring the internal element.
  • Applying a different transformation to Figure C.
  • Confusing rotation with reflection.
  • Choosing an option because it looks similar instead of checking the rule.
  • Missing small changes in direction or position.

⚡ Quick Solving Method

1. Compare A → B carefully.
2. Identify exactly what changed.
3. Check shape, direction, position and number of elements.
4. Apply the same change to C.
5. Compare the result with all four options.
6. Select the option with the exact same relationship.

📌 Quick Revision

  • Figure Analogy follows the pattern A : B :: C : ?
  • First identify the relationship between A and B.
  • Apply exactly the same relationship to C.
  • Check rotation, direction and position.
  • Check addition or removal of elements.
  • Do not choose an option merely because it looks similar.
