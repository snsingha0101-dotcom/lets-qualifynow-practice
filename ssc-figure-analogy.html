🔷 Figure Analogy

Understand the relationship between two figures and apply the same relationship to identify the correct corresponding figure.

What is Figure Analogy?

Figure Analogy is a non-verbal reasoning topic in which a relationship between two figures is given. You have to identify the relationship and apply the same rule to another figure to select the correct answer.

Basic Rule: A : B :: C : ?
The relationship between A and B must be the same as the relationship between C and the correct answer.

Important Types of Figure Analogy

1. Rotation

A figure rotates through a particular angle, such as 90°, 180° or 270°.

2. Reflection

A figure changes through a mirror image or reflection.

3. Position Change

The position of a dot, line or other element changes within a figure.

4. Addition of Elements

A new line, dot, shape or other element is added to the figure.

5. Removal of Elements

An element is removed while the remaining figure follows a rule.

6. Shape Transformation

A shape changes according to a specific and consistent pattern.

7. Direction Change

An arrow or another directional element changes its direction.

8. Combination of Rules

Two or more changes may occur together in the same analogy.

How to Solve Figure Analogy

1

Observe Figure A

Identify the important shapes, lines, dots and directions.

2

Compare A and B

Find the exact change between the two given figures.

3

Identify the Rule

Check rotation, reflection, movement, addition or removal.

4

Apply the Rule to C

Make the same change to the third figure.

5

Check the Options

Compare the resulting figure with all the answer choices.

6

Eliminate Incorrect Answers

Reject options that do not follow the identified relationship.

Common Mistakes

  • Checking only the outer shape and ignoring the smaller elements.
  • Confusing rotation with mirror reflection.
  • Ignoring the exact direction or angle of rotation.
  • Applying a different rule to the third figure instead of the original relationship.
  • Selecting an answer before comparing all the options.
  • Overlooking the movement or addition of a dot, line or other element.

Quick Solving Method

Remember this sequence:

Observe → Compare → Identify the Rule → Apply the Rule → Check the Options

In SSC exams, first check rotation, reflection, position change and addition or removal of elements. If no single rule fits, check whether the figure follows a combination of changes.

Quick Revision

  • Rotation: Check the angle and direction.
  • Reflection: Check whether the figure is reversed.
  • Position change: Track dots and other elements.
  • Addition or removal: Compare the number of lines and shapes.
  • Direction change: Observe the direction of arrows and symbols.
  • Final check: The same relationship must hold in both pairs.
