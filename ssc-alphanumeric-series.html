📘 SSC Reasoning – Alphanumeric Series

Complete concept-based preparation for Alphanumeric Series questions with concepts, position-based methods, examples, shortcuts and SSC-level practice.

📚 What is Alphanumeric Series?

An Alphanumeric Series is a sequence containing a combination of letters, numbers and symbols arranged according to a particular pattern or order.

In SSC Reasoning, questions are generally asked from the position, counting, arrangement, replacement and identification of elements in the given series.

Alphanumeric Series = Alphabet + Numbers + Symbols

🎯 Basic Concept

Consider the following series:

A 7 B 3 # C 9 D @ 5 E 2

Here the series contains three types of elements:

  • Alphabets: A, B, C, D, E
  • Numbers: 7, 3, 9, 5, 2
  • Symbols: #, @

Questions can ask you to count, identify or locate these elements according to their positions.

🔤 Alphabet Position

Alphabet positions are very important while solving Alphanumeric Series questions.

A = 1   B = 2   C = 3   ...   Z = 26

Forward Position

Count the letters from A towards Z.

Example:

What is the position of M?

Answer: 13

Backward Position

For reverse alphabet position:

Reverse Position = 27 − Forward Position
Example:

What is the reverse position of M?

13th letter M → 27 − 13 = 14

📖 Types of Alphanumeric Series Questions

1. Position-Based Questions

Questions based on the position of a particular letter, number or symbol.

2. Counting-Based Questions

Questions asking how many numbers, letters or symbols are present in a given series.

3. Number-Based Questions

Questions based on numbers appearing before, after or between particular elements.

4. Alphabet-Based Questions

Questions based on letters, their positions or their relationship with numbers.

5. Symbol-Based Questions

Questions based on the number and position of symbols.

6. Mixed / Combination Questions

Questions involving letters, numbers and symbols together.

7. Rearrangement-Based Questions

Questions in which elements are rearranged according to a given instruction before answering.

📍 Position of Elements

Consider the following series:

A 4 B 7 # C 2 D @ E 9
Question:

Which element is fourth from the left?

Answer: 7
Question:

Which element is third from the right?

Answer: @

🔢 Counting Elements

In counting-based questions, first identify the category asked in the question.

Series:

A 7 B # 4 C 9 D @ E 5

How many numbers are present?

Answer: 4

Numbers are 7, 4, 9 and 5.

Series:

A 7 B # 4 C 9 D @ E 5

How many symbols are present?

Answer: 2

Symbols are # and @.

↔️ Elements Between Two Elements

These questions ask how many elements are present between two specified elements.

Series:

A 7 B 3 # C 9 D @ E

How many elements are between B and D?

B → 3 → # → C → 9 → D

Answer: 4 elements

⬅️➡️ Before and After Questions

Some questions ask which number, letter or symbol comes immediately before or after a particular element.

Series:

P 5 Q @ R 8 S # T

Which element is immediately after R?

Answer: 8
Question:

Which element is immediately before S?

Answer: 8

🔠 Number and Alphabet Relationship

Sometimes numbers and letters are arranged in a specific relationship.

Example:

A 1 B 2 C 3 D 4

Here the alphabet and number positions correspond with each other.

A = 1, B = 2, C = 3, D = 4

Always check whether the given series contains a meaningful numerical or positional pattern.

🔣 Symbol-Based Questions

Symbols such as @, #, %, $, &, *, + and − may appear between letters and numbers.

Series:

A @ 5 B # 7 C % 9 D

How many symbols are present?

Answer: 3

In symbol-based questions, carefully count only the symbols specified in the question.

🔄 Rearrangement-Based Questions

In some questions, the series has to be rearranged according to a particular instruction.

Example:

A 5 B 8 C 2 D 6

If all numbers are arranged in ascending order, the numbers will become:

2, 5, 6, 8

Read the instruction carefully before rearranging any element.

🧠 How to Solve Alphanumeric Series Questions

Step 1 – Read the Complete Series

Do not start counting before understanding the complete arrangement.

Step 2 – Identify the Required Element

Determine whether the question is asking about a letter, number, symbol or position.

Step 3 – Count Carefully

Count from the correct direction — left or right — as mentioned in the question.

Step 4 – Apply the Instruction

If rearrangement or replacement is mentioned, perform that operation before answering.

Step 5 – Verify

Recheck the position or count before selecting the final answer.

Read → Identify → Count / Arrange → Solve → Verify

⭐ Important Points for SSC

  • Always read the entire series carefully.
  • Clearly identify whether the question asks about a letter, number or symbol.
  • For position questions, count from the correct side.
  • For questions involving "between", do not count the two given elements themselves.
  • Remember alphabet positions from A = 1 to Z = 26.
  • Use reverse alphabet positions when required.
  • In rearrangement questions, follow the instruction exactly.
  • Improve speed by practising mixed series regularly.

P 4 Q 7 R 2 S 9 T

How many numbers are present in the series?

A 5 B # C 8 D @ E 2

How many symbols are present?

A) 1    B) 2    C) 3    D) 4
Answer: B) 2

M 4 N 7 O 2 P 9 Q

Which element is immediately after O?

A) N    B) 7    C) 2    D) P
Answer: C) 2
Q5. Study the following series:

A 7 B 3 # C 9 D @ E

How many elements are between B and D?

A) 3    B) 4    C) 5    D) 2
Answer: B) 4
Q6. Study the following series:

A 1 B 2 C 3 D 4 E

What is the position of C in the alphabet?

A) 2    B) 3    C) 4    D) 5
Answer: B) 3
Q7. Study the following series:

A @ 5 B # 7 C % 9 D

How many symbols are present?

A) 2    B) 3    C) 4    D) 1
Answer: B) 3
Q8. Study the following series:

P 5 Q @ R 8 S # T

Which element is immediately before S?

A) 8    B) R    C) #    D) T
Answer: A) 8

⚡ Quick Revision

  • Alphanumeric Series = Letters + Numbers + Symbols.
  • Alphabet Position: A = 1 to Z = 26.
  • Reverse Position = 27 − Forward Position.
  • Position Questions require careful left/right counting.
  • Counting Questions require identification of the requested element type.
  • "Between" means count only the elements lying between the given elements.
  • In rearrangement questions, follow the instruction before answering.
  • Regular mixed practice improves speed and accuracy.
