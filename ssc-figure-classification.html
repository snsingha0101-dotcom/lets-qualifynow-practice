🔷 Figure Classification

Identify the figure that does not follow the common relationship among the given figures using shape, position, symmetry, direction and internal arrangement.

📘 What is Figure Classification?

Figure Classification is a non-verbal reasoning topic in which a group of figures is given and the student has to identify the figure that is different from the others according to a particular visual rule.

Important: The figures may look completely different. The classification is usually based on a hidden relationship such as rotation, symmetry, direction, number of elements, relative position or arrangement.

🎯 Important Features to Check

1. Shape Check the number and type of sides, curves and geometric elements.
2. Position Observe the position of dots, arrows, lines and inner shapes.
3. Direction Check whether arrows or triangular elements point in a particular direction.
4. Rotation Three figures may represent the same arrangement after rotation.
5. Symmetry Check vertical, horizontal or rotational symmetry.
6. Internal Relationship Compare the relationship between the outer and inner elements.

🧠 How to Solve Figure Classification

1 Observe all figures

Do not decide the answer after looking at only one option.

2 Compare structure

Check shape, direction and internal arrangement.

3 Check rotation

Ask whether the figures can become identical after rotation.

4 Find the common rule

Identify the relationship followed by three figures.

5 Find the exception

The figure that breaks the same rule is the answer.

B

Answer: C
A, B and D have a horizontal internal axis matching the horizontal symmetry of the outer figure. C changes the internal axis to vertical. Therefore C breaks the common relationship.

🔄 Rotation vs Reflection

This is one of the most important ideas in Figure Classification. A figure may look different simply because it has been rotated. Rotation does not change the relative order of its elements.

Rotation: The complete figure turns around a point while the relationship between its parts remains unchanged.

Reflection: The figure is flipped like a mirror. The left-right relationship changes.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Only comparing the outer shape.
  • Ignoring the position of the internal dot or line.
  • Considering a rotated figure as completely different.
  • Confusing rotation with mirror reflection.
  • Choosing an option before finding the common rule.
  • Checking only one feature instead of the complete relationship.

⚡ Quick Solving Method

Step 1: Look at the outer shape.
Step 2: Check internal lines and symbols.
Step 3: Check direction of arrows or pointed elements.
Step 4: Test rotation.
Step 5: Check symmetry and mirror relationship.
Step 6: Find the common relationship among three figures.
Step 7: Select the figure that violates that relationship.

📌 Quick Revision

  • Figure Classification is based on visual relationships.
  • Always compare all four figures.
  • Rotation does not necessarily make a figure different.
  • Mirror image changes left-right relationship.
  • Check both outer and inner elements.
  • Look for symmetry, direction and relative position.
  • The correct answer is the figure that breaks the established rule.
